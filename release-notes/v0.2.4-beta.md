# Studio 64 0.2.4-beta

New moves from other hacks, beta lighting and sounds for Mario, new shapes and full 3D rotation.

## Download

- `studio64-0.2.4-beta.zip`: Studio 64 itself, one ZIP for every system

You need your own Super Mario 64 ROM (US version). Studio 64 doesn't include the game.

Unzip it and start it:

- **Windows**: double-click **Start Studio 64**. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC",
  click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS**: right-click **Start Studio 64.command**, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
- **Linux**: run `./start-studio64.sh` in a terminal.

The first setup downloads about 1 GB (the compiler, the engine's source) and takes 5 to 10 minutes.
Already on an earlier version? Unzip 0.2.4 next to it and start it: the setup reuses what it
downloaded before.

## What's new

Thanks to [#8](https://github.com/supermochi64/studio64/issues/8) for all of these ideas.

- **New moves from other hacks.** Game rules → Moves → *New moves*: wall slide, spin jump (spin
  the stick and press A, or L + A), spinning triple jump, instant ground pound, ground pound jump,
  ground pound dive, ground pound out of any jump, underwater ground pound, dive hop and ledge
  vault. Each one switches on for the whole game or one level; *Modern moveset* turns them all
  on. Walking can be switched off too (Mario always runs). They work in the exported ROM.
- **Mario's lighting.** Assets → Mario → *Lighting*: how harsh the shading is on his face, his
  clothes and his hands and feet, and where the light comes from. *Beta* gives him the harsh look
  of the pre-release footage. His colours and lighting now go into the exported ROM too.
- **Every Mario sound can be replaced**, including the jump's "Wah" and "Hoo", the triple jump's
  "Waha" and "Yippee" and the footsteps on every kind of ground. **Beta sounds** plays an unused
  "Yah!" from the game's own voice bank.
- **New shapes**: inner and outer corners for ramps and stairs, a sphere, and a **terrain patch**
  you can place anywhere and sculpt like the ground. The pillar's texture no longer stretches.
- **Rotate on every axis**: the rotate tool (2) has X, Y and Z rings.
- **Notes play their full length** (they were cut off early), and you can **drag the playhead**
  in the composer to listen from any spot.

Everything else is in the [changelog](https://github.com/supermochi64/studio64/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

If the setup fails, please open an issue with the log: click **Show details**, then **Copy the
whole log** on the setup page. Please don't attach a ROM or a playable game export.
