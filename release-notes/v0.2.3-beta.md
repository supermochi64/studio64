# Studio 64 0.2.3-beta

Your music everywhere: replace any of the game's songs and jingles with your own, and start a hack
from scratch without the castle.

## Download

- `studio64-0.2.3-beta.zip`: Studio 64 itself, one ZIP for every system

You need your own Super Mario 64 ROM (US version). Studio 64 doesn't include the game.

Unzip it and start it:

- **Windows**: double-click **Start Studio 64**. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC",
  click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS**: right-click **Start Studio 64.command**, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
- **Linux**: run `./start-studio64.sh` in a terminal.

The first setup downloads about 1 GB (the compiler, the engine's source) and takes 5 to 10 minutes.
Already on an earlier version? Unzip 0.2.3 next to it and start it: the setup reuses what it
downloaded before.

## What's new

- **Replace a game song everywhere** ([#11](https://github.com/supermochi64/studio64/issues/11)).
  In the sound studio's *Game music* list, hover a song (say *Slider*), click **Replace** and
  pick one of your songs. It plays wherever the game plays that song: every level with slide
  music, the castle, the menus. A level that picked its own music keeps it. The composer's ⋯ menu
  has the same as *Play instead of a game song…*, and the ✕ under the song's name brings the
  game's song back. It works in the exported ROM too.
- **Fanfares and jingles**: star collect, star appears, puzzle solved, key and high score are
  replaced the same way, and your song plays once instead of looping.
- **From scratch means no castle.** A new hack from scratch has its first level as the start and
  the hub: stars, deaths and *Exit course* lead back there. The original levels are hidden from
  the level launcher, the warp pickers and *Starts in* (*World → Show the original levels* brings
  them back). Build your own levels and connect them with your own doors, pipes and paintings.
- Claude can replace game songs too (ask it, for example, to play your song instead of the slide
  music).
- Replaced sound effects work right after a reload again.

Everything else is in the [changelog](https://github.com/supermochi64/studio64/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

If the setup fails, please open an issue with the log: click **Show details**, then **Copy the
whole log** on the setup page. Please don't attach a ROM or a playable game export.
