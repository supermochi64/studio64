# Studio 64 0.2.2-beta

Bring your own music: the sound studio imports MIDI files now, and chords no longer lose notes.

## Download

- `studio64-0.2.2-beta.zip`: Studio 64 itself, one ZIP for every system

You need your own Super Mario 64 ROM (US version). Studio 64 doesn't include the game.

Unzip it and start it:

- **Windows**: double-click **Start Studio 64**. If SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC",
  click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS**: right-click **Start Studio 64.command**, choose **Open**, then **Open** again.
- **Linux**: run `./start-studio64.sh` in a terminal.

The first setup downloads about 1 GB (the compiler, the engine's source) and takes 5 to 10 minutes.
Already on an earlier version? Unzip 0.2.2 next to it and start it: the setup reuses what it
downloaded before.

## What's new

- **Import MIDI files** ([#9](https://github.com/supermochi64/studio64/issues/9)). Made a song in
  another music editor? In the sound studio, click **Import MIDI** (or drop the .mid file on it).
  Every part of the file gets one of the game's instruments, picked to match what it played (a
  bass gets a bass, drums the drum kit). Change any of them and hear it, choose the sound set, the
  timing (1/16, 1/32 or triplets), the bars to keep and the tempo, press **Listen**, then
  **Import**. The song opens in the composer, where you can still change everything.
- **Chords keep all their notes.** A track with more than four notes at once used to drop the
  rest; now they play on the game's spare music channels. The game itself sounds 16 notes at once
  (sound effects included), and the import tells you when a song has more.
- The composer's note lengths follow the song's grid, and the piano roll shows notes above C7 and
  below C2.

Everything else is in the [changelog](https://github.com/supermochi64/studio64/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).

If the setup fails, please open an issue with the log: click **Show details**, then **Copy the
whole log** on the setup page. Please don't attach a ROM or a playable game export.
