# Studio 64 trailer: credits

Everything in the trailer is real: the studio's interface, Claude's real replies (Opus 5.5 through the studio's chat: "give mario a car", "give mario a shotgun", "give mario a katana", "spawn bowser here and let me fight him here"), and the game running in the studio (filmed frame by frame from the engine; Mario is driven by a scripted controller). All four beats take place in the castle grounds. Score: promo/scripts/music4.py (cut: edit4.py, mix: mix3.py). The game sound (Bowser, the mines, Mario's "So long-a Bowser!") comes from the engine and a legally owned copy of Super Mario 64.
