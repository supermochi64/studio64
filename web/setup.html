Let's set up Studio 64

Studio 64 builds Super Mario 64 for your browser from your own copy of the game. You do this once and it runs by itself: the studio opens in about 5 minutes.

Updating? Bring over your old Studio 64

1

Your Super Mario 64 ROM

Required
2

Super Mario Sunshine characters

Optional

Piantas, Nokis, Strollin' Stus, Electrokoopas and more, converted from your own GameCube disc. You can also add them later.

Drop your ROM first
What happens next?
  • The compiler (Emscripten, about 400 MB) is downloaded once, unless you have it already. Tools your computer lacks (Python, a C compiler, make) are downloaded into this Studio 64 folder: nothing is installed on your system and you need no administrator password.
  • Your ROM's textures, sounds and levels are unpacked and the game is compiled to WebAssembly.
  • The studio opens. In the background it takes pictures of every object and level for the library and makes the sample hacks.
  • Everything stays on this computer: the ROM is stored in this Studio 64 folder (engine/baserom.us.z64) and is never uploaded anywhere.
