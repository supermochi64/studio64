# Tokyo template

Tokyo from the hack **Shoggoth World** as a level to start from: the night city with its neon and
Japanese signs, the scramble crossing, traffic, the commuter train, the shrine, the canal, the
construction site, its people and yokai, its night sky and its music. A new level made from it *is*
that Tokyo; build on it like on any level.

It ships with Studio 64 (Shoggoth World is its author's own hack): every checkout has the Tokyo card. Its
people and oni dressed on the game's Toad and Goomba are made from your ROM when the engine is built
(see [Building the engine](building-the-engine.md#the-shoggoth-content)).

## Using it

- **New level → Tokyo** (level launcher, `Ctrl+K`). The card shows the night sky with Tokyo's own
  city title card.
- The sample hack **Tokyo** on the home screen is the same level, ready to play
  (`npm run samples` makes it when the data is there).

What comes with the level:

| | |
|---|---|
| **The city** | One object, *Tokyo (the whole city)*: all of Shoggoth's level geometry with its textures, baked night lighting and ambient occlusion, additive neon glows, scrolling water and LED tickers, the far skyline; and its collision with the original surface types (no-camera surfaces, non-slip stairs) and the canal's water. It is level geometry: not picked by clicking in the level, but in the object list (**Objects → Scene**) where it can be moved or deleted; the collision follows. |
| **Scenery** | Traffic lights and pedestrian signals on one traffic cycle, giant screens with animated ads, flickering neon, the glass lifts, the canal boats, the construction crane's jib, falling petals and fireflies |
| **Traffic** | Taxis, kei cars, kei trucks and buses queue at red lights and stop short of Mario; the commuter train runs the elevated line |
| **People** | The crowd crosses the scramble when every signal shows WALK; the Toads of each district and the arrival sign say Shoggoth's texts (as Story dialogs and characters, editable in the Story panel); the Drift King and the stationmaster stand where they wait for Mario |
| **Enemies** | Oni and Ao-oni (Goombas in Tokyo's oni models), Chochin-obake lanterns, Kappa in the canal, Tanuki in disguise |
| **Stars** | *Construction Crane Climb* (the star on the crane's hook: the jib carries it round), *Red Coins All Over Tokyo* (eight red coins, the star on Hachiko Plaza) and the 100-coin star. Shoggoth's other Tokyo stars live in its sub-levels (the konbini, the maid café, the Hoshigaoka Line, the Shuto race) and are not part of the template. |
| **Coins, 1-ups, pipes** | All of the level's coin formations and 1-ups; the two warp pipes lead to each other |
| **Sky** | Shoggoth's night sky over Tokyo (moon, Mt. Fuji, skyline) at its full 496×496, as the level's sky asset (**Assets → Sky**) |
| **Music** | "Neon Crossing", Tokyo's own song, played by the game's sequence player with its instruments; in the level settings' music list as *Neon Crossing (Tokyo)*. Picking another song replaces it. |
| **Spawn, camera** | Mario starts at Tokyo's arrival point on Hachiko Plaza; the camera follows like in an open course |

Every one of the Tokyo objects is also in the build library (**Objects → Shoggoth**, `tokyo:*`), so
traffic lights, taxis, lanterns or a kappa can go into any level.

Not included: Shoggoth's story objects (Star Gates, glitches), the doors into its sub-levels, and its
night tint on Mario.

## How it is made

The shipped data was made from a Shoggoth World checkout (the `shoggoth` branch of
`sm64-master`) by `tools/import_shoggoth_tokyo.mjs`, as part of `npm run shoggoth:refresh`
(`tools/shoggoth/refresh.mjs`, a developer tool: it also takes the game's own data back out, see
[Building the engine](building-the-engine.md#the-shoggoth-content)). It only reads the checkout:

| From the checkout | Becomes |
|---|---|
| `levels/tokyo/` model, texture and geo data (generated by its `tools/shoggoth/courses/tokyo.py`) | `engine/src/studio/shoggoth/gen/tokyo/`: the display lists, collision and textures as C (PNG textures converted to the decomp's formats by `tools/shoggoth/n64tex.mjs`); the area's geo layout becomes `tokyo_city_geo` (the city without screen, camera and sky nodes) |
| `levels/tokyo/script.c` | `web/data/shoggoth/tokyo.json`: spawn, every object with its behavior parameters, warp nodes |
| `src/game/shoggoth/enemies_tokyo.c`, `data/sh_enemies_tokyo_bhv.inc.c` | `gen/enemies_tokyo*.inc.c`, the yokai's own code |
| `textures/skyboxes/tokyo_night.png`, `tools/shoggoth/intro_art/tokyo.png` | `web/data/shoggoth/tokyo_sky.png`, `tokyo_card.png` |
| `sound/sequences/us/24_custom_level_tokyo.s` | the song as `.m64` bytes in `tokyo.json` (assembled with the host's `cpp`, `as` and `objcopy`, or the checkout's own build of it) |
| `tools/shoggoth/music/bank.py` | `engine/sound/sound_banks/26_studio_tokyo.json`: the instruments from the game's own samples (bank 38) and the hack's synthesised ones (`engine/sound/samples/instruments/custom_sh_*.aiff`); the katana duel's battle theme plays on it too |
| `text/us/shoggoth_dialogs.h` | the Toads' and the sign's texts in `tokyo.json` |

All of it ships with Studio 64, except what is built on the game's own Toad and Goomba: the crowd's,
the yakuza's and the stationmaster's re-lit Toad triangles and recoloured caps and the oni's
recoloured Goomba faces are recipes (`gen/rom_recipes.json`) that every engine build turns into C from
your ROM (`engine/src/studio/romgen/tokyo/`). `tools/gen_catalog.py` adds the `tokyo:*` objects
(`gen/catalog.json`) to the catalog.

In the engine (`engine/src/studio/shoggoth/`):

- `tokyo_data.c` compiles the generated data (about 13 MB of C, +4 MB of wasm).
- `tokyo.c`: the city object (`bhvTkCity`), its collision added to the static collision at every
  rebuild (`studio_tokyo_add_surfaces`, offset by where the city stands) and its water boxes
  (`studio_tokyo_apply_water`), the texture scrolls, a rebuild when a city appears, moves or goes
  (`studio_tokyo_frame`), and Shoggoth's city life ported (traffic cycle, cars, train, lifts, boats,
  crane jib, petals, fireflies, pedestrians). Behavior scripts: `tokyo_behaviors.c`,
  `tokyo_enemies_bhv.c`.
- The engine's level bounds are ±16,384 (`LEVEL_BOUNDARY_MAX`, 32×32 collision cells), like
  Shoggoth World's: Tokyo reaches ±13,400.
- The renderer draws Shoggoth's additive glows (blender `CLR_IN·A_FOG + CLR_MEM`) additively
  (`gfx_pc.c`, `gfx_studio_set_additive`).
- `studio_sound_bank_count()` lets the page play the Tokyo song only when the bank is in the build.

In the web app: `web/js/tokyo_template.js` (`makeTokyoLevel` builds the area, its story dialogs and
characters; `createTokyoLevel` also uploads the sky), the card in `levels_ui.js`, the level song
setting `m64Song` in `world.js` and the music list (`editor_ui.js`).

The setup wizard's sample step makes the Tokyo sample (`tools/make_tokyo_sample.mjs`).

## Tests

`tests/suite/tokyo_template.test.mjs` (`node tests/run.mjs tokyo`): the card, the city's collision,
spawn, objects, sky, music and texts, views around the city, walking the plaza, crosswalks and the four
boulevards with the autopilot (`_studio_walk`), the station lift, the crane carrying its star,
deleting and restoring the city, and everything after a reload.
