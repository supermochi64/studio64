# Sandbox

The sandbox is a city game for the N64: open streets, cars and bikes, people, gangs, recruits,
shootouts, missions, and a hero with a 3D platformer's move set. It lives in
[`games/sandbox`](../games/sandbox/README.md), where you'll find how to play, build, test and change it.

## How it's made, and why

The first plan was to build the sandbox inside Studio 64's SM64 engine as one more ROM-export module.
That plan was dropped for a standalone game, for two reasons:

- **A ROM you can share.** An SM64 hack is a `.bps` patch, since the engine, the animations and the
  moves come from the player's own SM64 ROM. The sandbox contains no Nintendo code or data at all,
  so the finished `sandbox.z64` can be shared as it is. It still plays in every common N64 emulator,
  and on a console with a flash cart.
- **A connected city.** SM64's areas are about ±16,000 units, a few blocks at human scale. The
  sandbox streams a 1.3 km island in 64 m chunks (detail near, a simple model far away), like the
  big continuous worlds of recent N64 homebrew. A city can't be built that way out of SM64 areas.

The engine is written on [libdragon](https://github.com/DragonMinded/libdragon) (the open N64 SDK)
and [Tiny3D](https://github.com/HailToDodongo/tiny3d) (a fast 3D library for it). The platformer
moves (the jump chain, the long jump, the backflip, wall kicks, ground pounds, ledges, poles,
swimming) are our own implementation, made to feel like the classic 3D platformers. Every model,
texture, song and voice line is generated by scripts in `games/sandbox/tools/`.

## Hard rules it keeps

1. **N64 first.** It's an N64 ROM, tested in emulators (`tools/emu.mjs` runs ares and checks frame
   times). Nothing depends on a browser.
2. **None of Nintendo's content.** No code, models, textures, music or sounds.
3. **Easy to extend.** Weapons, vehicles, looks, songs, voices, districts and buildings are table
   entries or one function in the generator scripts.
4. **Not called GTA.** The game, the city (districts like Palm Court, Downtown, Sunset Beach), the
   gangs and the shops all have names of their own.
